01 — Philosophy

Markets drift. Principles hold.

We believe returns come from knowing more about less. Octant runs concentrated portfolios built through primary research — and holds them long enough for the work to matter.

01

Depth over breadth

Twenty-five names, not two hundred and fifty. Every position is underwritten as if we were buying the entire business — primary research, hundreds of conversations, models built from the unit economics up.

02

Time is the edge

Information advantages last weeks. Analytical advantages last months. Behavioral advantages — the willingness to look wrong for years — compound indefinitely. Our capital base is built to exploit the last one.

03

Risk is measured, never outsourced

No benchmark hugging, no factor crutches. We size positions so that being early — our most common error — is survivable, and being right is consequential.

An octant fixes a ship’s position from two constants: the horizon and a star. We navigate the same way — price is our horizon, value our star.
